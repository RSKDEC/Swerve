Answer sources and scoring

This pilot has 15 factual prompts across 3 days. The survey collection is retired from the current game.

What the index measures

English Wikipedia article views, all access, user traffic, 1 July–30 September 2026 (92 days). These are recorded article requests, not unique people or survey answers. Bots classified by Wikimedia are excluded by the user filter. Redirects, news events, language, and audience differences can affect attention; this index is a proxy, not a measure of what everyone knows.

Within each question, answers are ranked from least viewed (index 0) to most viewed (100), with tied counts sharing their average rank. Indices are independent and do not add up to 100. Displayed indices are rounded; scores use the unrounded rank. The snapshot is frozen for the entire challenge; today's players cannot change your score.

Points

Every listed correct answer earns 20 points plus up to 80 strategy points. Blend: 20 + 0.8 × index. Swerve: 100 − 0.8 × index. Sweet Spot: 100 in the 40–60 band, falling by 2 points per index step outside it to a minimum of 20. Round only the final score. Blank, timed-out and unlisted answers earn zero. There is no speed bonus, random jackpot or live AI judging.

Aliases are reviewed exact matches after case, accent, apostrophe and punctuation normalization. We do not guess near spellings. The lists are curated; omissions remain possible. An unlisted answer is not automatically factually wrong. This local pilot has no submission-review service or competitive leaderboard.

Sources and reuse

Prompts are original editorial wording. Factual membership lists link to pinned Wikipedia revisions, with Wikipedia contributors credited; source text is licensed under CC BY-SA. Wikimedia aggregate pageview data is available under CC0. See the Wikimedia Analytics API. This project is independent of Wikipedia, Nintendo, Disney and other referenced brands.
